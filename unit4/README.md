# Unit 4：Food and Meals

## 一、单元目标

本单元围绕饮食和三餐展开，目标是：

- 学习食物名称：bread, rice, milk, noodles, fruit
- 学习三餐：breakfast, lunch, dinner
- 讨论日常饮食：What do you have for breakfast?
- 运用一般现在时表达日常饮食习惯

## 二、核心课文

I usually have breakfast at seven in the morning. I eat bread and an egg. For lunch, I have rice and vegetables. My favourite food is noodles. I like fruit and milk very much.

### 重点句型

- I have breakfast at seven.
- I eat bread and an egg.
- What do you have for breakfast?
- My favourite food is noodles.

## 三、重点词汇

| 单词 | 中文 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| breakfast | 早餐 | I have breakfast at seven. |
| lunch | 午餐 | I have lunch at school. |
| dinner | 晚餐 | We eat dinner at home. |
| bread | 面包 | I eat bread every morning. |
| rice | 米饭 | Rice is delicious. |
| milk | 牛奶 | I drink milk every day. |
| fruit | 水果 | Apples are fruit. |
| noodles | 面条 | My favourite food is noodles. |

## 四、语法重点

### 1. 一般现在时

- I eat breakfast every morning.
- She drinks milk.
- We have lunch at school.

### 2. 喜好表达

- My favourite food is ...
- I like ... very much.

## 五、口语训练

- What do you have for breakfast?
- What is your favourite food?
- Do you like fruit?
- Do you like noodles?

- 能说出常见食物
