# Unit 5：Daily Life

## 1. 课文主题

本单元围绕日常活动、作息安排和时间表达展开。

### 课文示例

I get up at six thirty in the morning. I wash my face and brush my teeth. Then I eat breakfast and go to school. After school, I do my homework and play with my friends.

## 2. 重点词汇

- get up 起床
- wash my face 洗脸
- brush teeth 刷牙
- homework 作业
- after school 放学后
- evening 晚上
- time 时间
- clock 钟表

## 3. 语法点

### 时间表达

- at six thirty
- in the morning
- after school
- before dinner

## 4. 练习

### 选择题

1. I ___ up at six thirty.  
   A. get  B. gets  C. getting

2. I do my homework ___ school.  
   A. before  B. after  C. in

### 写作练习

写一段关于你一天生活的英语短文，至少 5 句。

## 5. 复习重点

- 会说日常作息
- 能用时间表达描述生活
- 能写简单的日常短文
