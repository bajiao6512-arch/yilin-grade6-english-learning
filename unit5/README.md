# Unit 5：Daily Life

## 一、单元目标

本单元围绕日常作息展开，帮助学生学习：

- 时间表达：at six thirty, in the morning
- 日常动词：get up, wash, brush, go, do
- 生活安排：morning, afternoon, evening
- 能写一段简单日常生活描述

## 二、核心课文

I get up at six thirty in the morning. I wash my face and brush my teeth. Then I eat breakfast and go to school. After school, I do my homework and play with my friends.

### 重点句型

- I get up at six thirty.
- I brush my teeth.
- I do my homework after school.
- I play with my friends.

## 三、重点词汇

| 单词 | 中文 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| get up | 起床 | I get up at six thirty. |
| brush teeth | 刷牙 | I brush my teeth every morning. |
| wash my face | 洗脸 | I wash my face before breakfast. |
| homework | 作业 | I do my homework after school. |
| evening | 晚上 | We have dinner in the evening. |
| time | 时间 | What time is it? |
| clock | 钟表 | The clock is on the wall. |
| after | 在……之后 | After school, I play football. |

## 四、语法重点

### 1. 时间表达

- at six thirty
- in the morning
- after school
- before dinner

### 2. 一般现在时用于日常活动

- I get up at six.
- She goes to school at eight.
- We do homework in the evening.

## 五、口语训练

- What time do you get up?
- What do you do in the morning?
- What do you do after school?
- Do you do homework every day?

## 六、练习题

### 选择题

2. I do my homework ___ school.

## 七、单元复习

- 能描述自己一天的安排
- 能使用时间表达说明日常活动
- 能写简单的 morning / evening 活动描述
