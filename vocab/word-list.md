# 词汇表

## 重点单词总表

| 单词 | 中文 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| school | 学校 | I go to school by bus. |
| teacher | 老师 | My teacher is kind. |
| family | 家庭 | This is my family. |
| brother | 兄弟 | I have a brother. |
| hobby | 爱好 | My hobby is reading. |
| breakfast | 早餐 | I have breakfast at seven. |
| lunch | 午餐 | We have lunch at school. |
| dinner | 晚餐 | Dinner is ready. |
| get up | 起床 | I get up at six. |
| homework | 作业 | I do my homework after school. |
| useful | 有用的 | English is useful. |
| culture | 文化 | We learn about culture. |

## 学习建议

- 每天背 5–10 个单词
- 用例句记忆单词
- 每周进行一次词汇复习
- 尝试用单词写简单句子
