# Unit 1：Welcome to Our School

## 1. 课文主题

本单元主要学习学校生活、班级介绍和基础问候句型。

### 课文示例

Hello, everyone! My name is Tom. I am eleven years old. I come to school by bus. This is my classroom. It is big and clean. My teacher is kind and friendly. I like English very much.

### 重点句型

- My name is ...
- I am ... years old.
- I go to school by ...
- This is my ...
- My teacher is ...

## 2. 重点词汇

- school 学校
- classroom 教室
- teacher 老师
- friend 朋友
- bus 公交车
- clean 干净的
- kind 和蔼的
- friendly 友好的

## 3. 语法点

### be 动词

- I am
- You are
- He/She/It is
- We are
- They are

例句：
- I am in Grade Six.
- She is my friend.

## 4. 练习

### 选择题

1. My name ___ Tom.  
   A. am  B. is  C. are

2. I go to school ___ bus.  
   A. by  B. on  C. in

### 填空题

1. This is my ________.
2. My teacher is very ________.

### 口语练习

- What is your name?
- How old are you?
- How do you go to school?

## 5. 复习重点

- 会说自己的姓名和年龄
- 会描述学校和老师
- 会使用基本的 be 动词
