# 语法总结

## 1. be 动词

- I am
- You are
- He/She/It is
- We are
- They are

示例：
- I am in Class 6.
- She is my sister.

## 2. There be 句型

- There is a book.
- There are two books.

示例：
- There is a big classroom.
- There are four people in my family.

## 3. 一般现在时

- I go to school by bus.
- She likes English.
- We have lunch at school.

## 4. like + 动词-ing

- I like reading books.
- He likes playing football.
- She likes drawing pictures.

## 5. 时间表达

- at seven o'clock
- in the morning
- after school
- before dinner

## 6. 常见句型

- What is your name?
- How old are you?
- How do you go to school?
- What is your hobby?
- What do you have for breakfast?

## 复习重点

- 语法要在句子里使用
- 多做练习，形成语言意识
- 牢记常见句型
