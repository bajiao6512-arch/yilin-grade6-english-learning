# Unit 3：My Hobbies

## 1. 课文主题

本单元学习兴趣爱好与日常活动表达。

### 课文示例

I like reading books. My brother likes playing football. My sister likes drawing pictures. We all have different hobbies. Doing what we like makes us happy.

## 2. 重点词汇

- hobby 爱好
- read 读书
- draw 画画
- play 玩
- football 足球
- music 音乐
- picture 图片
- different 不同的

## 3. 语法点

### like + 动词-ing

- I like reading.
- She likes drawing.
- He likes playing football.

## 4. 练习

### 选择题

1. I like ______ books.  
   A. read  B. reading  C. reads

2. He likes ______ football.  
   A. play  B. playing  C. plays

### 口语练习

- What is your hobby?
- Do you like swimming?
- What does your friend like doing?

## 5. 复习重点

- 会表达自己的兴趣爱好
- 能使用 like + 动词-ing
- 能简单谈论别人爱好
