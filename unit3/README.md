# Unit 3：My Hobbies

## 一、单元目标

本单元重点学习兴趣爱好和日常活动，学生可以学会：

- 表达爱好：reading, drawing, swimming, playing football
- 句型：I like ... / I like doing ...
- 喜欢和不喜欢表达：I like ... / I don't like ...
- 简单描述他人爱好

## 二、核心课文

I like reading books. My brother likes playing football. My sister likes drawing pictures. We all have different hobbies. Doing what we like makes us happy.

### 重点句型

- I like reading books.
- My brother likes playing football.
- My sister likes drawing pictures.
- We all have different hobbies.

## 三、重点词汇

| 单词 | 中文 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| hobby | 爱好 | Reading is my hobby. |
| read | 读书 | I like reading books. |
| draw | 画画 | She likes drawing. |
| play | 玩耍 | He likes playing football. |
| football | 足球 | I play football on weekends. |
| music | 音乐 | My hobby is listening to music. |
| picture | 图片 | She draws a picture. |
| different | 不同的 | We have different hobbies. |

## 四、语法重点

### 1. like + 动词-ing

- I like reading.
- I like swimming.
- She likes dancing.

### 2. 常见爱好表达

- play football
- read books
- draw pictures
- listen to music
- watch TV

## 五、口语训练

- What is your hobby?
- Do you like reading?
- What does your friend like doing?
- What do you do after school?

## 六、练习题

### 选择题

1. I like ______ books.  
   A. read  B. reading  C. reads

2. He likes ______ football.  
   A. play  B. playing  C. plays

### 填空题

1. My hobby is ______ pictures.
2. She likes ______ music.
3. We all have ______ hobbies.

### 口语作文

Write 5 sentences about your hobbies.

## 七、单元复习

- 能说出常见爱好
- 能用 like + 动词-ing 表达兴趣
- 能描述自己和他人的兴趣爱好
